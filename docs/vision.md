# AI Observatory — Vision

> **A personal, local-first system that observes the AI ecosystem every day, builds a record you can revisit at any time, and once a week distills it into a short list of the topics that actually matter—the research material for your content.**

---

## Why this exists

Keeping up with AI manually does not scale.

The most relevant developments—new models, research papers, product releases, funding, regulation, and open-source projects—are spread across dozens of trusted sources. Reviewing them every day is slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

AI Observatory exists to do the watching for you, so your attention goes to **understanding and creating**, not searching.

It remembers what happened, so you don't have to.

---

## What it does

The system operates on two simple rhythms.

### Every day — Observe and accumulate

Once a day, AI Observatory checks a curated list of trusted sources, identifies the day's significant AI developments, and stores them as a structured local record.

Each daily record is valuable on its own. If you want to know what happened on any given day, you can simply open it and read it.

Nothing is lost between runs—the record grows day by day.

### Once a week — Synthesize

At the end of the week, AI Observatory reviews everything it has accumulated and produces a short, ranked list of the week's most relevant topics.

Rather than summarizing every article, it answers a simpler question:

> **What changed this week, and why does it matter?**

That weekly briefing becomes the research material you use to create videos, articles, newsletters, presentations, or any other original content.

---

## What it is — and what it isn't

**AI Observatory is a monitoring system.**

It works proactively. It observes, records, and synthesizes information on a schedule without waiting for a question.

You consume the accumulated research instead of starting every investigation yourself.

**AI Observatory is not:**

- A chatbot
- A search engine
- A research-on-demand assistant
- An automatic content generator
- An auto-publisher

Its purpose is simple: prepare organized, trustworthy research material.

The human always decides what becomes content.

---

## Principles

### Local-first

Records and processing stay on your machine by default.

Cloud services are optional and intentional—not the default path.

### Trusted sources before volume

Coverage grows by adding carefully selected, high-quality sources rather than collecting everything available.

Quality beats quantity.

### Signal over noise

The goal is not to capture every AI headline.

The goal is to capture the developments that are actually worth understanding.

### Traceable

Every insight points back to its original source.

Nothing appears in a summary without evidence behind it.

### Daily record, weekly synthesis

The system observes every day and reasons once a week.

The value compounds over time.

### Human judgment is final

The system organizes and proposes.

The human interprets, creates, and decides.

Nothing is ever published automatically.

---

## Scope

AI Observatory observes developments across the AI ecosystem, including:

- Research, models, and APIs
- Open-source tools and developer ecosystems
- Products and user experience
- Business, funding, and industry movements
- Regulation and governance
- Social and professional impact

Coverage is determined by the trusted source list—not by these categories. The categories simply describe the breadth a good source list should achieve.

---

## Non-goals

AI Observatory is intentionally **not**:

- An automatic content generator or publisher
- A chatbot or research assistant
- A real-time alerting system
- A multi-user or SaaS product
- An exhaustive web crawler

It deliberately favors a small number of trusted, high-signal sources over complete coverage.

---

## Success Criteria

The project succeeds when, each week, you can:

1. Understand what happened on any day without researching it again.
2. Read one concise weekly briefing instead of dozens of scattered sources.
3. Trust every conclusion because every insight traces back to a real source.
4. Begin creating informed, original content directly from that briefing.

Everything in this project exists to serve that outcome.

Anything that does not contribute to it is out of scope.
